About Me

Hello! I'm Raoul Daril, a passionate Computer Science student with a love for building meaningful digital experiences. This project, Discover Cameroon, was created as part of my school curriculum to combine my interest in tourism, technology, and user-centered design.

I believe that technology can bridge the gap between people and the hidden gems of our beautiful country. My goal is to make exploration accessible, intuitive, and enjoyable for everyone — whether you're a local or a visitor.

The Project

Discover Cameroon is a web application designed to help users explore tourist sites, cultural landmarks, and natural wonders across Cameroon. It's built with modern web technologies and focuses on:

  • Clean & responsive interface
  • Geolocation & maps integration
  • Rich multimedia content (videos, photos)
  • Multi‑language support (FR & EN)

Key Features

Modern Design

Clean, responsive interface optimized for both desktop and mobile devices.

Smart Search

Reliable and fast place search to quickly find destinations.

Rich Places

Add and display tourist sites with descriptions, categories, difficulty, danger level, and estimated price.

Multimedia Content

Videos, photos, and detailed information for each location.

Geolocation

Find your current position and explore nearby places with ease.

Directions

Get turn‑by‑turn directions between your location and any destination.

Place Details

Dedicated page with full info, location, and multimedia for each site.

Bilingual

Available in both French and English for a wider audience.
